K-Nearest Neighbors Simulator

Select a class, click to add training points, then click Classify Query Point and click once on the canvas.


Red points and Blue points are training data. The black cross is the new query point.

Formula used

Euclidean distance between query point \(Q(x_q,y_q)\) and point \(P(x_p,y_p)\):

d(Q,P) = √[(xq − xp)² + (yq − yp)²]
