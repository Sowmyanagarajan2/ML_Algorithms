🧠 Interactive K-Means Clustering

Customer segmentation using only Age and Annual Income. No labels are given to the algorithm.

What students should observe:
1. K = number of groups we want.
2. Each customer is assigned to the nearest centroid.
3. Centroids move to the average of their assigned customers.
4. Repeat until the groups stop changing.
